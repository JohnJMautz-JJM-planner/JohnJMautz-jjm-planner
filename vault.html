The Signal Tower

"My last message went out from this tower, scrambled so only a patient mind could read it."

You've climbed to the top of the Keeper's signal tower. Search the room. Tap anything that catches your eye. Somewhere up here are a scrambled message, the tool to read it, and the key to set that tool.
Episode 2

The Supper Table

"Four guests came to my last supper. One of them sat where only I am meant to sit."

The Keeper's dining room, the morning after. Four guests: Ivy, Moss, Rook and Sable. Who sat in the Keeper's own chair? Search the room for what the guests left behind.

The seating plan

The table seen from above. Tap a guest, then a chair, to try a seating. Tap a clue in your notebook to cross it off. The table never tells you if you're right.
